About Me

A data-focused graduate who turns spreadsheets into decisions.

I'm a computer applications graduate currently pursuing my MCA, with hands-on experience in MIS reporting, Excel, dashboards, data analysis and reporting. I enjoy the moment a messy spreadsheet turns into a clean, readable report — that's the work I do best.

My goal is simple: help small businesses and companies convert raw, scattered data into useful reports, automated spreadsheets and easy-to-understand dashboards, so decisions can be made faster and with more confidence.

I'm currently building my freelance practice while continuing my studies, which means I bring genuine attention, structured thinking and up-to-date tools — without the overhead of a full-time hire.
