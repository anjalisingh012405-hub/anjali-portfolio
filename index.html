About Me

Lead Tracking Dashboard

Business Problem
Leads came in from multiple sources (ads, IndiaMART, direct) with no unified view of conversion, revenue or which showroom/adviser was performing — follow-ups and reporting were scattered.
My Solution
Built a Google Apps Script web dashboard reading live from the master lead sheet — KPI summary, month-wise and showroom-wise trends, source and status breakdowns, and a leaderboard, all filterable by month, status, city and category.
Key Features
  • Total, converted & lost leads + revenue KPIs
  • Showroom-wise & month-wise trend charts
  • City-wise & lead-source pie charts
  • Lead status distribution (won/lost)
  • Multi-filter slicers (month, status, city, category)
  • Top-performer leaderboard

P&L / MIS Dashboard

Business Problem
Profitability data was spread across separate sheets for sales, expenses, manufacturing cost, stock and payments — no single statement tied it all together for month-end review.
My Solution
Built a comprehensive Apps Script dashboard that reads and reconciles multiple source sheets into one P&L statement — monthly trend, annual summary, top gross-profit items, manufacturing cost by model, and stock and payment reconciliation, all in one place.
Key Features
  • Monthly sales vs expenses vs investment
  • Net surplus % trend & annual P&L statement
  • Top 20 items by gross profit
  • Operating expense break-up
  • Model-wise manufacturing cost
  • Payment-vs-receipt & stock reconciliation

Payment Collection & Pending Tracker

  • Custom date-range filtering (week/month/custom)
  • Bill-level days-pending calculation

  • All-time vs period-based pending views

MIS Hub — Multi-Dashboard Apps Script Platform
